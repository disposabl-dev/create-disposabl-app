# Publish to Google Play

Start with the [shared release checklist](README.md), then use Play Console:

- [ ] Create the app record and upload a signed Android App Bundle. Publish to an [internal test track](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334) and test the installed app and its key flows.
- [ ] Complete the store listing and app content declarations, including accurate data safety and account access instructions. Give reviewers working credentials and access to provider-dependent and paid features.
- [ ] Configure products, subscriptions and matching backend notifications if used. Use [license testers](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/test) to test the genuine Play purchase flow and verify entitlements. Test-track users who are not license testers can incur real charges.
- [ ] Meet any applicable testing and production access requirements, then submit a production release for review. An internal test publication is not a production submission.
- [ ] Monitor review messages and publishing status; check managed publishing and rollout settings before announcing availability.

Consult the [Google Play policy center](https://play.google.com/about/developer-content-policy/) for current privacy, account deletion, AI and payment requirements.
